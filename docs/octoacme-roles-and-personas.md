# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security Champions

### Role Summary
Security Champions help the team identify and reduce security risk early in the lifecycle. They partner with engineering, product, and project stakeholders to ensure security considerations are built into planning and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review features and workflows for security risks and compliance requirements
- Define and maintain security review checkpoints for major changes
- Partner with engineers on secure design, testing, and remediation planning
- Escalate high-risk issues to Product Lead, Sponsor, or Security stakeholders as needed
- Support secure release readiness and incident response coordination

### Goals
- Reduce preventable security issues and vulnerabilities
- Align delivery with policy and compliance requirements
- Build a security-aware engineering culture

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and design discussions
- Risk and dependency tracking in project updates
- Escalation to PMs and leadership during critical issues

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to review risk and remediation plans
- Provides input to Product Managers on risk trade-offs and prioritization
- Coordinates with Project Managers to ensure security checkpoints are included in schedules and milestones
- Supports Stakeholders by surfacing security implications of release or roadmap decisions

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads turn business and product signals into actionable insights. They help teams track outcomes, monitor adoption, and connect execution to measurable value.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain core success metrics and reporting views
- Monitor feature adoption, quality, and operational signals
- Translate usage data into recommendations for backlog prioritization
- Partner with Product Managers and PMs on milestones and stakeholder updates
- Support decision-making with evidence from dashboards and experiments

### Goals
- Improve visibility into product performance and customer impact
- Ensure decisions are informed by measurable outcomes
- Reduce guesswork in roadmap and release planning

### Typical Communication
- Weekly reporting and dashboard reviews
- Product and roadmap discussions
- Stakeholder updates on trends, risks, and opportunities

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers on metric definitions and success criteria
- Shares evidence with Project Managers for reporting and risk assessments
- Collaborates with Developers to validate instrumentation and data quality
- Keeps Stakeholders informed on how delivery is impacting business goals

---

## Customer Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Success Representatives connect the delivery team with customer realities, feedback, and adoption issues. They help ensure the roadmap and release plans reflect customer value and readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Capture feedback from customers, support teams, and user-facing teams
- Identify pain points, unmet needs, and adoption risks
- Share customer context with Product Managers and developers during planning and delivery
- Support launch readiness and post-release feedback loops
- Help prioritize issues that affect customer trust, retention, or usability

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Turn customer feedback into actionable product decisions
- Reduce friction between product delivery and customer experience

### Typical Communication
- Customer interviews, feedback loops, and support escalations
- Cross-functional planning sessions with Product and Delivery teams
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and release readiness

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides insight to Product Managers for prioritization and roadmap refinements
- Helps Project Managers identify communication needs and release risks
- Partners with Developers to clarify user pain points and acceptance scenarios
- Keeps Stakeholders aligned on customer impact and support implications

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the operational side of shipping changes to production. They help balance readiness, communication, and execution discipline across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule and coordinate release windows and deployment activities
- Confirm readiness criteria, smoke tests, and rollback plans are in place
- Align dependencies across engineering, QA, support, and stakeholder teams
- Communicate release status, risks, and known issues to relevant audiences
- Support incident recovery or rollback when a deployment performs poorly

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational surprise
- Improve predictability of production changes
- Ensure stakeholders are informed before and after deployment

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and readiness reviews
- Deployment notifications and post-release follow-ups
- Escalation to PMs and Sponsors when release risk is elevated

### Interaction with existing roles
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on deployment readiness and validation
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing with milestones and communication plans
- Supports Product Managers with launch communications and customer-facing impact summaries
- Provides Stakeholders with clear release status and rollback guidance

---

## QA Leads / Quality Owners

### Role Summary
QA Leads help ensure the team ships software that meets quality expectations, acceptance criteria, and operational standards. They serve as a quality partner across planning, implementation, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, validation scope, and critical quality checkpoints
- Review acceptance criteria and ensure they are testable
- Coordinate manual, automated, and regression testing activities
- Triage defects and quality risks with engineering and product stakeholders
- Support release readiness decisions and sign-off criteria

### Goals
- Increase confidence in product quality and stability
- Catch issues early and reduce post-release defects
- Align quality standards with business and customer expectations

### Typical Communication
- Sprint review, QA planning, and defect triage sessions
- Release readiness reviews and quality sign-off discussions
- Escalation to PMs and Product Leads when quality risks affect delivery

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation against acceptance criteria
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm user expectations and release quality standards
- Provides Project Managers with risk awareness and validation status for milestones
- Shares release quality signals with Stakeholders and supports go/no-go decisions

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify ownership, accountability, and handoffs across project planning, delivery, quality, release, and stakeholder communication.
